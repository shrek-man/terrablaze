THERMALTRACE — INDIA-WIDE SATELLITE FIRE MONITORING

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Double-click RUN_WEBSITE.bat and keep its terminal open.
3. Wait for the browser to open at http://127.0.0.1:8000.
4. The app starts in **Demo only** mode with synthetic sample points, not live NASA data. If the terminal shows `GET /api/demo`, it is serving this sample data.
5. For live data, get a NASA FIRMS MAP_KEY, paste it into the key field or choose a .txt file containing only the key, then click Load live data.
6. Choose Recent for near-real-time feeds or Historical to select a start date and inspect the standard archive. “All available satellite feeds” combines every feed NASA lists for that period.

To fetch NASA data, save your MAP_KEY and click **Load live data**. The terminal should then show `POST /api/analyze`. Nearby detections are grouped into hotspot histories; select a row to zoom to it and see observation dates, satellites, measurements, classification evidence, and distances to nearby mapped industrial/power sites. NASA's rectangular results are clipped to India's boundary. Overpass searches are limited to areas around current hotspots and split into smaller batches; previously returned OSM features are cached locally for later requests.

The app builds a local historical baseline from earlier FIRMS detections you have loaded. It compares matching satellite overpasses at the same hotspot, day/night period, and similar UTC hour. Three earlier active dates are required. FIRMS supplies hotspot detections, not a continuous record of factory temperatures or confirmed zero-fire days.

Labels are candidate categories, not field-confirmed causes. Distance is straight-line to mapped sites, and satellite points are approximate pixel centers. The key stays in this browser tab's session storage; use Clear saved key to remove it.
